About AI.Reply

AI.Reply is a browser-based software product (Chrome extension, Manifest V3) built and operated by an individual developer based in India. It integrates directly with Gmail to help professionals, freelancers, and small teams manage their inbox faster — auto-tagging emails, summarizing them in one line, and generating context-aware replies with a single click.

Business Use-Case

AI.Reply is a SaaS product distributed as a Chrome extension. We offer a freemium subscription: a free 30-day trial (50 AI replies/day) followed by a paid Pro plan (500 AI replies/day) billed monthly or annually. Customers are individual professionals worldwide who use Gmail for business or personal email correspondence.

What it does

  • Auto-tags emails (Urgent, Finance, Meeting, Follow-up)
  • One-line AI summary per email

  • Guide AI — refine replies in plain English
